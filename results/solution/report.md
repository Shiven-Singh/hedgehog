# Clause review

## 2 Themart Com Inc: Agency Agreement

### Anti-Assignment

**Present, subject to an exception**

Section 6.5 prohibits the sublicensing, transfer, or assignment of intellectual property rights granted under the agreement, but explicitly allows third-party web hosting and certain sublicensing by 2TheMart to business affiliates.

> All rights (under any applicable intellectual property right) granted herein are not sublicenseable, Source: 2THEMART COM INC, 10-12G, 8/26/1999 transferable or assignable.

*Contract text, S008.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> Notwithstanding the foregoing, either party may use a third party web host, but all actions or failures to act of the web host that would be a breach of this Agreement, were the actions or failures to act taken by the applicable party, shall be deemed a breach of this Agreement. In addition, 2TheMart may grant sublicenses to companies that 2TheMart has a business relationship with to the extent that 2TheMart Content is visible from such company's web-site through a link or other means.

*Contract text, S008.*

### Change Of Control

**Present, subject to an exception**

Section 8.3 grants either party the right to terminate upon a change of control (acquisition of a majority of equity securities by another company), subject to an express carve-out for certain internal transactions involving i-Escrow, i-Escrow.com, and existing shareholders.

> If a majority of the equity securities of either 2TheMart or i-Escrow, Inc. (except that i-Escrow may sell all or a majority of its equity securities or voting interests to i-Escrow.com, and i-Escrow.com may sell all or a majority of its equity securities or voting interests to i-Escrow's existing shareholders, without triggering the foregoing) are acquired by another company during the term of this Agreement either company may terminate this Agreement, without liability, by giving a thirty (30) days written notice to the other party.

*Contract text, S010.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> except that i-Escrow may sell all or a majority of its equity securities or voting interests to i-Escrow.com, and i-Escrow.com may sell all or a majority of its equity securities or voting interests to i-Escrow's existing shareholders, without triggering the foregoing

*Contract text, S010.*

### Exclusivity

**Present, subject to an exception**

Section 2.4 restricts i-Escrow from running banner ads for competitors on the Co-Branded Site, qualified by the requirement that 2TheMart provide a written exclusion list identifying those competitors.

> i-Escrow shall not run banner advertisements on the Co-Branded Site for any of 2TheMart's competitors.

*Contract text, S004.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> 2TheMart shall provide in writing, a list of companies they would like to exclude, including every time they wish to change this list.

*Contract text, S004.*

Detto is prohibited from assigning rights or delegating obligations without obtaining PivX's prior written consent.

> Detto may not assign any of its rights or delegate any of its obligations hereunder, whether by operation of law or otherwise, without PivX's prior written consent.

> without PivX's prior written consent

**Not found**

The contract does not contain a change of control provision.

### Exclusivity

**Present, subject to an exception**

Detto has an exclusive distribution license within North America, but its distribution license outside of North America is explicitly non-exclusive.

> Subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement, PivX hereby grants to Detto a non-transferable, exclusive license to distribute PivX's Qwik-Fix Pro and any documentation supporting Qwik-Fix Pro provided from time to time by PivX (the "Documentation") within North America, solely to third parties to whom Detto licenses Qwik-Fix Pro ("Third Parties"), and as governed by the terms set forth in Exhibit A (PivX/Detto Reseller Agreement Addendum).

*Contract text, S001.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> PivX also hereby grants to Detto a non-transferable, non-exclusive license to distribute PivX's Qwik-Fix Pro and the Documentation outside of North America, solely to Third Parties and as governed by the terms in Exhibit A.

*Contract text, S001.*

### Requires a reader

**No clause located:** Change Of Control. The review did not find this clause, which is not the same as the contract being silent on the point.

## Allied Esports Entertainment Inc: Sponsorship Agreement

### Anti-Assignment

**Present, subject to an exception**

The general prohibition on assignment without prior written consent is qualified by an exception for transfers to successor entities pursuant to mergers, consolidations, or sales of substantially all assets or equity.

> Neither Newegg nor Allied shall have the right or power to assign or transfer any part of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior consent in writing of the other Party.

*Contract text, S008.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> The parties acknowledge that the transfer of this Agreement to a successor entity in connection with a merger, consolidation, or the sale of all or substantially all of the assets or equity of a party constitutes a continuation of the existing contractual relationship and not an assignment, transfer or delegation for the purposes of this Agreement. The consent requirements set out in this Agreement have no application to any such transaction, and no notice to the other party is required in respect of it.

*Contract text, S009.*

### Change Of Control

**Not found**

The agreement does not contain a standard change of control provision governing termination, consent, or notification rights upon a change of control.

### Exclusivity

**Present, subject to an exception**

The exclusivity obligations are qualified both by a pre-existing arrangements carve-out and by express exceptions for incidental third-party endorsements not controlled by Allied (e.g., individual player and team sponsorships).

> Allied shall not endorse, or permit the marketing of any other company whose principal business is as an e-commerce provider at or in connection with the Arena.

*Contract text, S003.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> The parties acknowledge that any product, service, territory or customer relationship in existence as at the Effective Date lies outside the scope of the undertakings given under this Agreement in respect of exclusivity, restricted dealing, or minimum commitment. The continuation, renewal or expansion of any such arrangement by either party is permitted and shall not constitute a breach of this Agreement.

*Contract text, S009.*

### Requires a reader

**No clause located:** Change Of Control. The review did not find this clause, which is not the same as the contract being silent on the point.

*Contract text, S019.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> KI, Inc. may assign its rights hereunder, but shall furnish written notice of such assignment to Diplomat.

*Contract text, S019.*

### Change Of Control

**Not found**

The contract contains no change of control provisions.

> The license hereby granted shall be exclusive as to the products described in subparagraphs 2.(a)(1) and (2) of this Agreement, but nonexclusive as to all other products covered by this Agreement.

## Aurasystemsinc: Strategic Alliance Agreement

### Anti-Assignment

**Present, subject to an exception**

While assignments generally require prior written approval, transfers to successor entities via mergers, consolidations, or sales of substantially all assets or equity are specifically exempted from consent and notice requirements.

> Except as expressly provided for herein, neither party may assign or otherwise transfer any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the other party's prior written approval and any such assignment or transfer shall be void.

*Contract text, S011.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> The parties acknowledge that the transfer of this Agreement to a successor entity in connection with a merger, consolidation, or the sale of all or substantially all of the assets or equity of a party constitutes a continuation of the existing contractual relationship and not an assignment, transfer or delegation for the purposes of this Agreement. The consent requirements set out in this Agreement have no application to any such transaction, and no notice to the other party is required in respect of it.

*Contract text, S012.*

### Change Of Control

**Present, unqualified**

Section 7.2(a) grants either party the right to terminate the agreement if the ownership or operations of the other party materially change or if all/substantially all assets are transferred.

> At the election of either party, in writing, if: (i) all or substantially all of the assets of the non-terminating party are transferred, sold or liquidated; (ii) the non-terminating party is dissolved; (iii) the non-terminating party is adjudged a bankrupt or becomes insolvent; (iv) the non-terminating party enters into an assignment or other arrangement for the benefit of its creditors; or (v) the ownership or operations of the non-terminating party have materially changed;

*Contract text, S009.*

### Exclusivity

## Berkshire Hills Bancorp Inc: Endorsement Agreement

### Anti-Assignment

**Present, subject to an exception**

While Berkshire generally cannot assign the agreement without written consent, Section 2(c) creates an express exception allowing assignment/conveyance of License Rights upon a merger with another entity offering Banking Services, and Section 22 permits Auriemma to assign monetary benefits.

> Berkshire may not assign this Agreement, in whole or in part, without Auriemma's written consent. In the case of reorganization, merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of its assets, any attempt to assign this Agreement other than as permitted above will be null and void.

*Contract text, S009.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> The License Rights are exclusive to Berkshire and may not be assigned or in any way conveyed by Berkshire without Auriemma's express written consent, except in the event of a merger by Berkshire with another entity offering Banking Services.

*Contract text, S002.*

### Change Of Control

**Present, unqualified**

The continuity of ownership provision specifies that internal reorganizations or transactions where beneficial ownership remains substantially unchanged do not trigger change-of-control rights or obligations, with no conflicting limitations elsewhere in the contract.

> For the purposes of this Agreement, a transaction in which the ultimate beneficial ownership of a party remains substantially unchanged, including any internal reorganisation, recapitalisation, or transaction effected solely to alter the jurisdiction of incorporation of that party, does not constitute a change of control, an acquisition, or a transfer of control of that party. The rights and obligations arising under this Agreement upon a change of control have no application to any such transaction.

*Contract text, S010.*

### Exclusivity

**Present, subject to an exception**

Auriemma's exclusivity covenant is qualified by an express carveout regarding any arrangements or agreements entered into by the University of Connecticut with a Berkshire competitor.

> Auriemma will not enter into any arrangement or agreement, which enables any Berkshire Competitor to be endorsed by Auriemma (whether by using the Auriemma Identification, Auriemma providing services similar to the Endorsement Services, or otherwise) during the Contract Period within the Contract Territory.

*Contract text, S003.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> Notwithstanding the foregoing, it is understood that Auriemma has no control or influence over any decisions by the University of Connecticut to enter into any arrangement or agreement with any Berkshire Competitor.

*Contract text, S003.*

## Conformis Inc: Development Agreement

### Anti-Assignment

**Present, subject to an exception**

Assignment without prior written consent is restricted, but permitted without consent to Affiliates or in connection with a sale of substantially all assets/business (including via Change of Control or merger), subject to specified conditions.

> Except as otherwise provided herein, a Party shall not have the right to assign any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement (whether through a merger, sale of stock, or otherwise) without the prior written consent of the other Party;

*Contract text, S024.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> except that, either Party shall be permitted, without any need for the other Party's consent, to assign this Agreement (a) in whole or in part to an Affiliate (provided, however, that once such Person is no longer an Affiliate of the assigning Party, such former Affiliate shall assign this Agreement back to the assigning Party), provided that the assigning Party provides the other Party notice of any such assignment provided further that failure to provide such notice of such assignment shall not render such assignment void; or (b) to a Third Party in connection with sale or transfer of all or substantially all of the assigning Party's business or assets relating to the subject matter of this Agreement, whether by Change of Control, merger, sale of assets or otherwise;

*Contract text, S024.*

### Change Of Control

**Present, subject to an exception**

Change of control provisions and restrictions on transfers are qualified by the Continuity of Ownership clause, which excludes internal reorganizations, recapitalizations, or reincorporations where beneficial ownership remains substantially unchanged.

> Except as otherwise provided herein, a Party shall not have the right to assign any of its rights or obligations under this Agreement (whether through a merger, sale of stock, or otherwise) without the prior written consent of the other Party; except that, either Party shall be permitted, without any need for the other Party's consent, to assign this Agreement (a) in whole or in part to an Affiliate (provided, however, that once such Person is no longer an Affiliate of the assigning Party, such former Affiliate shall assign this Agreement back to the assigning Party), provided that the assigning Party provides the other Party notice of any such assignment provided further that failure to provide such notice of such assignment shall not render such assignment void; or (b) to a Third Party in connection with sale or transfer of all or substantially all of the assigning Party's business or assets relating to the subject matter of this Agreement, whether by Change of Control, merger, sale of assets or otherwise;

*Contract text, S024.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> Continuity of Ownership. For the purposes of this Agreement, a transaction in which the ultimate beneficial ownership of a party remains substantially unchanged, including any internal reorganisation, recapitalisation, or transaction effected solely to alter the jurisdiction of incorporation of that party, does not constitute a change of control, an acquisition, or a transfer of control of that party. The rights and obligations arising under this Agreement upon a change of control have no application to any such transaction.

*Contract text, S024.*

### Exclusivity

**Present, subject to an exception**

The exclusivity restriction prohibiting development of Patient-Specific Instrumentation for third parties contains exceptions for Conformis's and its acquirers'/affiliates' own implants and certain patent licenses/immunities.

> Except as specifically provided in the Distribution Agreement, Conformis shall be prohibited from developing or assisting another in developing, or causing another to develop, Patient-Specific Instrumentation for Off-The-Shelf Knee Implants for any Third Party in the field of orthopedics until January 1, 2032 (or earlier, to the extent set forth in Section 2.3.3.4 or Section 2.3.5 of the Distribution Agreement), with the exception that Conformis (including any entity involved in a Change of Control of Conformis, any such entity an "Acquirer"), may develop Patient-Specific Instrumentation for any Off-The- Shelf Implants of Conformis, an Acquirer or any of their Affiliates.

*Contract text, S010.*

The following passage qualifies the clause above:

> with the exception that Conformis (including any entity involved in a Change of Control of Conformis, any such entity an "Acquirer"), may develop Patient-Specific Instrumentation for any Off-The- Shelf Implants of Conformis, an Acquirer or any of their Affiliates. For purposes of clarity, the foregoing does not prevent Conformis from granting any license, release, covenant not to sue or other immunity to any third party under any Patents, including any such immunity that would authorize manufacture, use or sale of Patient-Specific Instrumentation for Off-The-Shelf Knee Implants outside the Buyer Field.

*Contract text, S010.*
